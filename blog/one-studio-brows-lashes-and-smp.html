Brows, lashes and SMP under one roof

Why it helps to have your brow artist, lash artist and hair loss specialists in the same studio.

Most people see one person for lashes, another for brows and a completely different office if they ever deal with hair loss. At the Delaware SMP studio in Wilmington, those services live under one roof.

What is in the building

  • Lashes: extensions, fills and lash lifts with tint by Kelsey Tyre of K.A.T Eyes
  • Brows: microblading and permanent makeup, also by Kelsey
  • Scalp micropigmentation: for men and women with thinning hair, balding or scars, through Delaware SMP

Why that is useful

Your face reads as a whole. Brows frame your eyes, lashes finish them, and your hairline frames everything. When the people working on each part share a space, it is easier to get a look that fits together.

It is also practical. One address, one parking lot, one phone number. If you come in for lashes and have questions about thinning hair, you can ask in the same visit.

Pigment work is a specialty

Microblading and scalp micropigmentation both place pigment in the skin, but they are different crafts. Brows need fine strokes that look like hair. SMP uses tiny dots that look like hair follicles. Working in a studio built around pigment work means the standards for hygiene, color matching and aftercare are the same across the board.
