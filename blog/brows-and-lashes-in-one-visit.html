Booking brows and lashes in one visit

Want brows and lashes done together? What to book first, how long it takes and how to plan it at the Delaware SMP studio.

Brows and lashes frame your whole face, so it makes sense to plan them together. Here is how to book them so they work with each other.

Brows usually come first

Your brow shape changes how your eyes look. If you are getting microblading or a big brow change, do that first. Then your lash artist can pick a lash map that matches your new brows.

Can you do both the same day?

For a lash lift and a brow tint, often yes. For microblading and a full set of extensions, it is usually better to split them up. Both take time, and your face needs to stay clean and dry after microblading.

A simple plan

  1. Free consultation for brows and lashes together.
  2. Microblading or brow service first.
  3. Lash set a week or two later, once your brows have settled.
  4. Regular lash fills, plus a brow touch up at six to eight weeks.
