Microblading vs ombre brows

Hair strokes or a soft fade? How microblading and ombre brows compare on look, skin type and how long they last.

Microblading and ombre brows are two of the most popular semi-permanent brow options. They give very different results, so it helps to know the difference before you book.

The look

Microblading draws fine hair strokes by hand. It looks like natural brow hair.

Ombre brows use tiny pigment dots for a soft fade, lighter at the front and darker at the tail. It looks like nicely filled-in brows.

Skin type

  • Normal to dry skin: either works, and microblading strokes stay crisp.
  • Oily skin: ombre usually heals better, because strokes can blur.

How long it lasts

Microblading usually lasts around one to two years. Ombre brows often last longer. Both fade over time and need a touch-up, and sun, skin care and oily skin can speed up fading.

Upkeep

Both need a touch-up visit about six to eight weeks after the first session, then a refresh down the road. Aftercare is similar: keep them clean and dry while they heal and do not pick.

Quick guide

  • Want the most natural look? Microblading.
  • Like a made-up brow, or have oily skin? Ombre.
  • Want both? Ask about combination brows.
